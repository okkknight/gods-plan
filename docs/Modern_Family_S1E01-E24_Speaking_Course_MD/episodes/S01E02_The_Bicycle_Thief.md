## 1. 中文剧情描述

这一集叫《偷自行车的人》，三条故事线都在讨论父母为了孩子会做什么，以及他们即使出发点是好的，也常常把事情弄得一团糟。

第一条故事线是菲尔和卢克。卢克一直骑姐姐的旧自行车，因为克莱尔认为他还不够负责，不能拥有自己的新车。菲尔不顾克莱尔的反对，给卢克买了一辆新自行车，希望给他一次证明自己的机会。后来，菲尔在路边看到一辆没有上锁、外形完全相同的自行车，便认定卢克把新车随手丢在了那里。为了给儿子一个教训，他把那辆车骑走，想让卢克以为车被偷了。

回家途中，菲尔帮助漂亮的女邻居黛丝蕾翻窗进入她的房子。等他出来时，自行车却不见了。为了掩盖自己的错误，他又买了一辆一模一样的车。回家后他才发现，卢克的自行车一直都在家里，他偷走的其实是别人的车。克莱尔发现事情后，本来觉得很好笑，也愿意原谅他，但菲尔仍然撒谎说车是在加油站丢的。就在克莱尔快要相信他时，黛丝蕾把车送了回来，还提到菲尔曾进入她的卧室。最后，菲尔试图把错误拿走的车放回原处，却被真正的车主发现并追赶。

第二条故事线是杰伊和曼尼。格洛丽亚让他们一起安装吊扇，希望两人能够拉近关系。杰伊拒绝看说明书，曼尼则一直提醒他注意安全，两人很快发生争吵。后来，曼尼的亲生父亲哈维尔因为在赌场赌博，临时取消了带他去迪士尼乐园的计划。杰伊非常生气，却不愿让曼尼失望，于是编了一个善意的故事，并把自己和格洛丽亚原本要使用的豪华轿车改成了全家去迪士尼的交通工具。

第三条故事线是米切尔和卡梅隆。他们带莉莉参加亲子活动。米切尔太在意别人如何看待他们，要求卡梅隆收敛自己。看到别的孩子比莉莉表现得更好后，他们甚至把另一个孩子搭好的积木说成是莉莉完成的，随后才发现教室里有摄像头。米切尔也意识到，自己为了融入别人，反而让卡梅隆不能做真实的自己。

这一集最后的观点是：父母不可能永远做对，但一个好父亲最重要的事情，往往是在孩子真正需要他时出现。

## 2. Spoken English 标准版

I’d like to introduce Episode 2 of *Modern Family* Season 1. It is called **“The Bicycle Thief.”** All three storylines explore the same question: what does it mean to be a good father?

The first story is about Phil and Luke. Luke has been riding his sister’s old bike because Claire does not think he is responsible enough to own a new one. Phil disagrees and buys him a brand-new bike, hoping that Luke will prove he can take care of it.

Later, Phil sees an identical bike sitting on the street without a lock. He assumes that Luke has left his new bike there, so he takes it to teach him a lesson. On his way home, Phil stops to help their attractive new neighbor, Desiree, climb through a window because she has locked herself out. When he comes outside again, the bike is gone.

Phil buys another identical bike to cover up the problem. However, when he gets home, he discovers that Luke’s real bike has been there the whole time. He has taken the wrong bike. Claire works out what happened and finds it funny, but Phil still claims that the bike disappeared at a gas station. Then Desiree returns it and mentions that Phil was in her bedroom, exposing his lie. In the end, Phil tries to put the original bike back, but its real owner catches him.

The second story is about Jay and Manny. Gloria asks them to install a ceiling fan together so that they can become closer. Jay ignores the instructions, while Manny keeps warning him about safety. They argue and say some hurtful things. Later, Manny’s biological father, Javier, cancels their Disneyland trip because he is gambling at a casino. Jay is furious, but he does not want Manny to feel rejected. He invents a kinder explanation and uses the limousine meant for his own trip to take Manny and Gloria to Disneyland.

The third story is about Mitchell and Cameron. They take Lily to a parent-and-baby class. Mitchell is nervous about fitting in and asks Cameron to tone himself down. When the other children seem more advanced than Lily, they pretend that Lily has stacked another child’s blocks. They quickly discover that the class is recorded on camera. Mitchell also realizes that his insecurity has made him dishonest and has stopped Cameron from being himself.

Overall, the episode shows that parents often make mistakes while trying to do the right thing. As Jay says near the end, ninety percent of being a dad is just showing up.

## 4. 本集可迁移口语表达

- **be responsible enough to do something** — 足够负责，可以做某事
- **teach someone a lesson** — 给某人一个教训
- **cover up a mistake** — 掩盖错误
- **have been there the whole time** — 一直都在那里
- **show up for someone** — 在某人需要时出现并支持他

### 建议练习

1. 菲尔的故事按“买车 → 拿错车 → 丢车 → 买替代品 → 谎言被揭穿”五步练习。
2. 杰伊的故事重点练习转折：两人先争吵，但后来杰伊真正承担了父亲的责任。
3. 米切尔和卡梅隆的故事不必讲太多课堂细节，讲清楚“想融入 → 做错事 → 接受真实的彼此”即可。
4. 复述结尾时，用一句话回答：What does it mean to be a good father?
