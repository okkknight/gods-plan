# S01E18 — Starry Night

## 1. 中文剧情描述

这一集围绕孩子们的学校作业展开。卢克要做一个关于梵高的展示，菲尔自告奋勇帮助他，却总被各种小事分心。克莱尔本来负责帮助海莉为学校活动做纸杯蛋糕，却逐渐发现海莉只是想让母亲替自己完成全部工作。克莱尔为了让女儿承担责任，故意退出。海莉只能自己动手，做出的蛋糕非常糟糕，却也第一次真正完成了自己的任务。卢克的展示虽然看起来古怪，最后却意外地完成得不错。

杰伊、米切尔和曼尼一起到户外观星。米切尔希望借这个机会和父亲相处，但杰伊不断拿他小时候缺乏男子气概的事情开玩笑。曼尼也因为自己的兴趣和性格被取笑。米切尔起初很生气，后来却发现自己和曼尼有很多相似之处，并开始保护和鼓励他。杰伊也逐渐放下嘲讽，三个人最终度过了一个真正亲近的夜晚。

卡梅隆和格洛丽亚则单独出去吃饭。卡梅隆想证明自己能吃辣，结果被辣得几乎无法正常活动。格洛丽亚照顾他，两个人也在购物、衣服和家庭话题上找到共同语言。米切尔原本担心他们没有话聊，事实却证明，他们比想象中更合得来。

这一集讲的是陪伴孩子完成事情与替孩子完成事情之间的区别，也讲了成年人如何在放下刻板印象以后，发现彼此真正的共同点。

## 2. Spoken English 标准版

I’d like to introduce Episode 18 of *Modern Family* Season 1. It’s called **“Starry Night.”** The episode is about helping family members without taking over their responsibilities.

Luke has to prepare a school presentation about Vincent van Gogh. Phil volunteers to help, but he keeps getting distracted and does not provide much useful support. Claire is supposed to help Haley make cupcakes for a school event. She gradually realizes that Haley expects her to do all the work. Claire refuses to take over, so Haley has to make the cupcakes herself. They turn out terribly, but she finally takes responsibility for her own assignment. Luke’s presentation looks strange and disorganized, yet he manages to complete it in his own way.

Meanwhile, Jay, Mitchell, and Manny go outside to look at the stars. Mitchell hopes to have a meaningful evening with his father, but Jay keeps joking about how unathletic and sensitive Mitchell was as a child. Manny is teased for similar reasons. Mitchell first becomes defensive, then realizes that Manny reminds him of himself. He begins supporting Manny instead of letting Jay embarrass him. Jay eventually becomes less sarcastic, and the three of them finally share a genuine moment together.

In the third storyline, Cameron and Gloria go out for dinner. Cameron tries to prove that he can handle extremely spicy food, but it quickly overwhelms him. Gloria takes care of him, and the two discover that they have plenty to talk about, especially fashion, shopping, and family. Mitchell had worried they would have nothing in common, but they get along surprisingly well.

Overall, the episode is about giving people room to do things in their own way. Support is useful, but control often prevents real growth and connection.

## 4. 本集可迁移口语表达

- **take over a responsibility** — 把别人的责任接过来
- **take responsibility for something** — 为某事负责
- **see oneself in someone** — 在某人身上看到自己
- **have something in common** — 有共同点
- **get along well** — 相处融洽
