# S01E19 — Game Changer

> 用途：中文理解剧情 → 学习 Spoken English 标准版 → 使用 Cue Version 脱稿复述  
> 英文长度：约 **293 词**；正常学习语速约 **2.0–2.4 分钟**

## 1. 中文剧情描述

这一集发生在菲尔生日当天，而这一天正好也是新款 iPad 发售的日子。菲尔非常想成为第一批拿到它的人，克莱尔答应替他排队购买，却不小心睡过头。她跑遍各家商店，发现早已售罄。为了不让菲尔失望，她整天想办法寻找一台。最后，卢克通过网络联系到一个买家，并告诉对方自己的父亲病得很重，成功拿到了 iPad。菲尔完全不知道其中的夸张故事，只觉得这是自己最棒的生日礼物。

杰伊和曼尼下国际象棋。杰伊把自己当成高手，不愿输给孩子。曼尼其实比他强，却在格洛丽亚的暗示下故意隐藏实力，以保护杰伊的自尊。后来曼尼决定认真比赛，并赢走了杰伊的一块手表。杰伊以为自己终于遇到真正的对手，却不知道格洛丽亚才是家里最厉害的棋手。

米切尔和卡梅隆通过婴儿监听器意外听见邻居夫妻争吵。卡梅隆忍不住介入，试图修复他们的婚姻。米切尔觉得这种行为越界，也担心自己在别人眼中不够有男子气概，于是做出一些笨拙的“强硬”表现。卡梅隆的干涉虽然让人尴尬，最后却真的让邻居重新交流。

这一集的“游戏改变者”既是新科技，也是每个人突然改变局面的举动。很多计划都不是靠成熟的方法完成的，却意外得到了想要的结果。

## 2. Spoken English 标准版

I’d like to introduce Episode 19 of *Modern Family* Season 1. It’s called **“Game Changer.”** The episode takes place on Phil’s birthday, which is also the release day of the first iPad.

Phil desperately wants the new device and dreams of getting one on the first day. Claire promises to wait in line for him, but she accidentally oversleeps. By the time she reaches the stores, the iPad is sold out everywhere. She spends the day searching because she does not want to disappoint Phil. Luke eventually finds a seller online. To persuade the man to give them the device, Luke claims that his father is seriously ill and that the iPad is his final wish. The story is completely dishonest, but it works. Phil receives the iPad and believes his family has given him the perfect birthday.

Meanwhile, Jay plays chess with Manny. Jay sees himself as an expert and hates losing. Manny is actually a stronger player, but Gloria encourages him to hide his ability to protect Jay’s pride. Manny finally decides to play seriously and wins one of Jay’s watches. Jay is impressed to have found a worthy opponent, although he still does not know that Gloria is the best chess player in the house.

Mitchell and Cameron accidentally hear their neighbors arguing through the baby monitor. Cameron becomes emotionally involved and tries to repair their marriage. Mitchell thinks he is crossing a line. He also worries that the neighbors do not see him as masculine enough, so he makes several awkward attempts to act tougher. Cameron’s interference is inappropriate, but it surprisingly helps the couple communicate.

Overall, the episode shows how an unexpected person or action can completely change a situation, even when the method is far from perfect.

## 4. 本集可迁移口语表达

- **be sold out** — 售罄
- **persuade someone to do something** — 说服某人做某事
- **protect someone’s pride** — 照顾某人的自尊
- **find a worthy opponent** — 遇到值得较量的对手
- **cross a line** — 越界

### 建议练习

1. 先看中文，确认人物关系和故事顺序。
2. 跟读英文标准版，重点模仿停顿和连接方式，不要逐句翻译。
3. 只看 Cue Version 完整复述；卡住时先绕开表达，不立刻查看原文。
4. 复述结束后对照标准版，只修改影响表达清晰度的问题。
