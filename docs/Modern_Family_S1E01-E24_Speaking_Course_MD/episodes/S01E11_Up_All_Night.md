# S01E11 — Up All Night

## 1. 中文剧情描述

这一集里，曼尼的亲生父亲哈维尔突然出现。他总是迟到、没有计划，却很会讲故事，也很有魅力。杰伊原本非常不喜欢他，认为他不负责任，但两个人因为棒球和摩托车聊得投机，杰伊很快也被他吸引。格洛丽亚看得很清楚：哈维尔擅长让所有人开心，却不擅长真正承担责任。果然，他再次没有兑现对曼尼的承诺。杰伊这才意识到，自己差点被同样的魅力骗过去。

菲尔突然肾结石发作，疼得几乎无法动弹。克莱尔打电话叫来消防员帮忙，却特意换了一身更好看的衣服，因为她觉得其中一位消防员很有吸引力。菲尔虽然痛苦，还是敏锐地发现了这一点。到了医院，他一边接受治疗，一边因为克莱尔的表现吃醋，让原本紧张的情况变得十分滑稽。

另一边，米切尔和卡梅隆试图训练莉莉独立入睡。米切尔坚持不能一听到哭声就进去抱她，卡梅隆却完全无法忍受。两个人整晚轮流阻止对方，也都被哭声折磨得筋疲力尽。米切尔甚至在黑暗中受了伤，但仍努力证明自己的方法是对的。

这一集的几条故事线都和“忍耐”有关：忍受疼痛、忍受孩子哭泣，也忍受一个总让人失望却很难讨厌的人。

## 2. Spoken English 标准版

I’d like to introduce Episode 11 of *Modern Family* Season 1. It’s called **“Up All Night.”** As the title suggests, almost everyone has a long and exhausting night.

Manny’s biological father, Javier, suddenly arrives. He is unreliable and rarely follows a plan, but he is charming and full of exciting stories. Jay dislikes him at first because he believes Javier keeps disappointing Manny. However, the two men begin talking about baseball and motorcycles, and Jay quickly gets caught up in Javier’s energy. Gloria is not impressed. She knows that Javier is good at making promises but bad at keeping them. Sure enough, he lets Manny down again. Jay finally understands why Gloria does not trust him, even though he is so easy to like.

At the Dunphy house, Phil has a severe kidney stone and can barely move. Claire calls the fire department for help, but before the firefighters arrive, she changes into a more attractive outfit because she remembers that one of them is handsome. Phil is in terrible pain, but he still notices. At the hospital, he becomes jealous while also acting as if he is about to die, turning a painful emergency into a comic performance.

Meanwhile, Mitchell and Cameron try to teach Lily to fall asleep by herself. Mitchell wants to let her cry for a while, but Cameron cannot stand listening to her. They spend the night arguing, checking on Lily, and stopping each other from going into the room. Both are exhausted, and Mitchell even hurts himself in the dark, but neither wants to admit defeat.

Overall, this episode is about patience. The characters have to endure physical pain, a crying baby, and a charming person who keeps letting the family down.

## 4. 本集可迁移口语表达

- **follow a plan** — 按照计划行事
- **get caught up in something** — 不知不觉被某事吸引或卷入
- **keep a promise** — 信守承诺
- **let someone down** — 让某人失望
- **admit defeat** — 承认失败
