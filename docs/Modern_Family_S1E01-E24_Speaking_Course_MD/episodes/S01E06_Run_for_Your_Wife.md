# S01E06 — Run for Your Wife

> 用途：中文理解剧情 → 学习 Spoken English 标准版 → 使用 Cue Version 脱稿复述  
> 英文长度：约 **289 词**；正常学习语速约 **2.0–2.4 分钟**

---

## 1. 中文剧情描述

这一集发生在孩子们开学后的第一天。三个孩子都去上学后，克莱尔原本以为自己终于能轻松一下，但她很快发现，家里突然安静下来反而让她有些不习惯。菲尔误以为她需要鼓励，主动提出和她比赛跑步，想证明两个人仍然年轻有活力。克莱尔其实比菲尔跑得快，但最后故意让他赢。就在菲尔得意的时候，正在学车的海莉开车撞到了他。

另一条故事线是曼尼第一天上学的造型。他坚持穿一件传统披肩，还准备在学校吹排箫。杰伊担心他因此被同学嘲笑，于是偷偷把披肩拿走。格洛丽亚发现后要求杰伊尊重曼尼的选择，并把衣服还给他。不过，当曼尼真的开始练习刺耳的排箫时，连格洛丽亚也受不了了，私下要求杰伊想办法让乐器消失。

与此同时，米切尔和卡梅隆发现莉莉的头撞了一下，两个人立刻陷入恐慌，带她去看医生。他们一路担心别人会认为他们不是称职的父母，也把医护人员的普通反应误解成歧视。医生确认莉莉没事后，他们终于放松下来，却又不小心把她锁在车里，只能再次求助。

这一集把父母的焦虑表现得很真实：他们都想保护孩子，但常常分不清，自己是在帮助孩子，还是在试图控制孩子。

## 2. Spoken English 标准版

I’d like to introduce Episode 6 of *Modern Family* Season 1. It’s called **“Run for Your Wife.”** The episode takes place on the first day of school.

After all three children leave the house, Claire expects to enjoy the peace and quiet. Instead, the empty house makes her feel a little emotional. Phil completely misreads the situation. He thinks Claire needs excitement, so he challenges her to a running race. Claire is actually faster, but she lets Phil win because she knows how much it means to him. Just as Phil is celebrating, Haley, who is learning to drive, accidentally hits him with the car.

The second storyline is about Manny’s first day at school. He wants to wear a traditional poncho and play the pan flute for his classmates. Jay is worried that the other children will make fun of him, so he secretly takes the poncho away. Gloria finds out and insists that Jay return it. She believes Manny should be free to express himself. However, when Manny starts practicing the extremely annoying flute, Gloria quietly asks Jay to make that part of the outfit disappear.

Meanwhile, Mitchell and Cameron panic after Lily bumps her head. They rush her to the doctor and worry that everyone will think they are careless parents. They also misunderstand several normal comments and assume that people are judging them as a gay couple. The doctor tells them that Lily is completely fine. They finally relax, only to lock her inside the car by accident a few minutes later.

Overall, the episode is about anxious parents trying to do the right thing. Their intentions are good, but fear and pride often make simple situations much more complicated than they need to be.

## 4. 本集可迁移口语表达

- **peace and quiet** — 安静清闲
- **misread the situation** — 误判情况
- **let someone win** — 故意让某人赢
- **make fun of someone** — 嘲笑某人
- **make a situation more complicated** — 把事情弄得更复杂

### 建议练习

1. 先看中文，确认人物关系和故事顺序。
2. 跟读英文标准版，重点模仿停顿和连接方式，不要逐句翻译。
3. 只看 Cue Version 完整复述；卡住时先绕开表达，不立刻查看原文。
4. 复述结束后对照标准版，只修改影响表达清晰度的问题。
