# S01E04 — The Incident

## 1. 中文剧情描述

这一集围绕杰伊和格洛丽亚婚礼上发生过的一场家庭冲突展开。杰伊的前妻迪迪来到镇上，声称自己想为当时失控的行为向大家道歉。米切尔提前知道她要来，却只告诉了杰伊，没有告诉格洛丽亚，因为他担心两人再次打起来。

一家人后来聚在一起吃饭。起初，迪迪表现得很平静，也认真向格洛丽亚道歉，气氛似乎终于缓和下来。但她很快又开始失控，甚至扑向格洛丽亚。场面一片混乱，最后反而是海莉的男朋友迪伦说了一番意外成熟的话，让所有人暂时冷静下来。大家第一次觉得，迪伦也许并没有看起来那么傻。

与此同时，海莉想和迪伦一起去听演唱会。菲尔和克莱尔原本不太放心，于是认真观察迪伦是否值得信任。迪伦在饭桌上的表现让他们一度改观，他们甚至准备允许海莉和他出去。然而，迪伦随后唱了一首自己创作的歌，歌词越来越露骨，菲尔和克莱尔立刻收回了许可。

另一边，格洛丽亚得知米切尔故意瞒着自己后非常生气。她决定用一个小恶作剧来报复他，让米切尔明白，被家人蒙在鼓里的感觉并不好受。

这一集展示了这个家庭的旧矛盾还没有真正消失，但也说明，最不被看好的人有时反而会在混乱中说出最有道理的话。

## 2. Spoken English 标准版

I’d like to introduce Episode 4 of *Modern Family* Season 1. It’s called **“The Incident.”** The title refers to a huge family fight that happened at Jay and Gloria’s wedding.

Jay’s ex-wife, Dede, comes back to town and says she wants to apologize for losing control at the wedding. Mitchell knows that she is coming, but he only warns Jay. He does not tell Gloria because he is afraid the two women will fight again.

Later, the whole family has dinner together. At first, Dede seems calm and gives Gloria a sincere apology. For a moment, everyone thinks the problem has finally been settled. However, Dede soon becomes emotional again and attacks Gloria. The dinner turns into complete chaos. Surprisingly, Dylan, Haley’s boyfriend, is the one who speaks up and tells the adults how ridiculous they are being. His comments are simple but honest, and Phil and Claire suddenly see him in a better light.

This connects to the second storyline. Haley wants permission to go to a concert with Dylan. Phil and Claire are not sure whether they can trust him, so they spend the evening judging his behavior. After Dylan handles the family fight so maturely, they decide that he may be more responsible than they thought. Unfortunately, he then performs a song he wrote for Haley. The song starts out sweet, but the lyrics become more and more inappropriate. Phil and Claire immediately change their minds about the concert.

Meanwhile, Gloria is furious when she learns that Mitchell kept Dede’s visit a secret from her. She gets back at him with a prank, reminding him that nobody likes being left in the dark.

Overall, this episode is about old family conflicts coming back to the surface. It also shows that the person everyone underestimates may sometimes be the most sensible person in the room.

## 4. 本集可迁移口语表达

- **lose control** — 失去控制
- **settle a problem** — 解决一个问题
- **see someone in a better light** — 对某人改观
- **leave someone in the dark** — 对某人隐瞒情况
- **get back at someone** — 报复或回敬某人
