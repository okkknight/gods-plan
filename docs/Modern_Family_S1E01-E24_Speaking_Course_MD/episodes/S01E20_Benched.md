# S01E20 — Benched

> 用途：中文理解剧情 → 学习 Spoken English 标准版 → 使用 Cue Version 脱稿复述  
> 英文长度：约 **294 词**；正常学习语速约 **2.0–2.5 分钟**

## 1. 中文剧情描述

这一集从卢克和曼尼的篮球比赛开始。球队教练脾气暴躁，总是对孩子们大喊大叫。菲尔和杰伊一起要求他改变方式，教练却当场辞职，球队突然没有人带领。菲尔和杰伊都觉得自己最适合接任，于是从合作变成竞争，不断争夺教练的位置。他们越想证明自己，越忽略场上的孩子。最终，两个人不得不承认，这场比赛不应该成为成年人争夺权威的舞台。

克莱尔带亚历克斯去购物，却在遇到亚历克斯的同学后被女儿迅速抛下。亚历克斯更愿意和朋友一起逛街，让克莱尔第一次强烈感到女儿进入了青春期。格洛丽亚也遇到同样的问题：曼尼告诉她，在朋友面前表达过多的爱意会让自己难堪。两个母亲后来一起散步并照看莉莉，互相安慰，也开始接受孩子需要逐渐与父母拉开一点距离。

另一边，米切尔失业在家，卡梅隆则去贺卡公司工作，两个人交换了原来的家庭角色。米切尔想念职场，卡梅隆想念陪伴莉莉，但他们都假装对新安排很满意。米切尔后来得到一份工作机会，两人终于承认都想恢复原来的生活。可他们在离开时不小心被锁在车库里，还弄坏了新老板的法拉利。老板于是告诉米切尔，他现在更必须来上班，才能赔偿损失。

这一集讲的是角色变化带来的不适。无论是父母、教练还是伴侣，人很容易把“被需要”当成自己的身份，一旦位置变化，就会感到失落。

## 2. Spoken English 标准版

I’d like to introduce Episode 20 of *Modern Family* Season 1. It’s called **“Benched.”** The episode is about adults struggling when their usual role is taken away or begins to change.

Luke and Manny’s basketball coach constantly yells at the children and makes cruel comments. Phil and Jay ask him to treat the team better, but he suddenly quits in the middle of a game. The team now needs a coach, and both Phil and Jay want the position. They begin competing for control instead of working together. Each man wants to prove that he understands the game and the children better. Their argument becomes more important to them than the actual players, until they finally realize that the game should not be about adult pride.

Meanwhile, Claire takes Alex shopping. When Alex sees some school friends, she quickly leaves Claire to join them. Claire feels rejected because her daughter is becoming a teenager and no longer wants her mother around all the time. Gloria has the same experience when Manny asks her not to be so affectionate in front of his friends. The two mothers walk together while taking care of Lily and admit that their children are beginning to need more independence.

Mitchell is still unemployed, while Cameron now works for a greeting-card company. Their usual roles have reversed. Mitchell misses the workplace, and Cameron misses staying home with Lily, but both pretend to be happy. Mitchell receives a job offer, and they finally admit that they want their old arrangement back. Then they accidentally damage the new boss’s Ferrari, making the decision much less optional.

Overall, the episode shows how strongly people connect their identity to a role. When that role changes, even a good change can feel like a loss.

## 4. 本集可迁移口语表达

- **compete for control** — 争夺控制权
- **feel rejected** — 感到被拒绝或被冷落
- **need more independence** — 需要更多独立空间
- **reverse roles** — 交换角色
- **connect one’s identity to something** — 把自我身份与某事绑定
