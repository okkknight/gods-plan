## 1. 中文剧情描述

这一集发生在圣诞节前。菲尔和克莱尔发现沙发上有一个烧焦的小洞，认定是三个孩子中有人偷偷抽烟造成的。孩子们都不承认，菲尔一气之下宣布取消圣诞节，并把所有装饰和礼物都收了起来。他原本以为严厉的惩罚会逼出真相，但孩子们一直沉默。亚历克斯最后为了让家人恢复过节，主动承担了并不是自己做的错事。后来大家才发现，烧焦沙发的其实是阳光经过圣诞装饰反射造成的。菲尔和克莱尔只好承认自己误会了孩子，并用一次去意大利旅行的承诺弥补过度惩罚。

杰伊家里则在争论应该怎样过圣诞节。杰伊希望格洛丽亚和曼尼完全按照美国传统来庆祝，而他们也想保留哥伦比亚的节日习惯。双方一开始互相嫌弃，最后却把两种传统放在了一起，包括很不安全但十分热闹的烟花。

米切尔和卡梅隆在商场拍莉莉与圣诞老人合影时，嫌扮演圣诞老人的人太瘦，结果导致对方被解雇。他们感到内疚，便邀请那个人来家里吃饭。但事情很快变得尴尬，他们发现自己的善意也带着不少先入为主的判断。

这一集用几场误会说明：节日最容易让人追求“正确的家庭气氛”，但真正的家庭往往是在犯错、道歉和妥协中一起过节。

## 2. Spoken English 标准版

I’d like to introduce Episode 10 of *Modern Family* Season 1. It’s called **“Undeck the Halls,”** and it is the show’s first Christmas episode.

At the Dunphy house, Phil and Claire find a burn mark on the sofa. They assume that one of the children has been smoking, but Haley, Alex, and Luke all deny it. Phil decides to cancel Christmas until somebody tells the truth. He removes the tree, the decorations, and the presents, expecting the guilty child to confess. Nobody does. Alex finally takes the blame even though she is innocent because she does not want the whole family to lose Christmas. Later, they discover that sunlight reflected through an ornament caused the burn. Phil and Claire realize they punished the children for something they did not do, so Phil tries to make up for it by promising a family trip to Italy.

At Jay’s house, Jay wants Gloria and Manny to celebrate Christmas in a traditional American way. Gloria and Manny want to include Colombian customs as well. At first, both sides treat the other traditions as strange. Eventually, they combine them, ending with loud and slightly dangerous fireworks.

Meanwhile, Mitchell and Cameron take Lily to meet Santa at the mall. They complain that the Santa is too thin and accidentally get him fired. Feeling guilty, they invite him to dinner. Their attempt to help becomes awkward because they keep making assumptions about him and his life.

Overall, the episode shows how quickly people can become controlling during the holidays. Everyone wants the “right” kind of Christmas, but the family only enjoys it after they admit their mistakes and make room for one another’s traditions.

## 4. 本集可迁移口语表达

- **take the blame** — 替别人或为某事承担责任
- **make up for something** — 弥补某事
- **make assumptions about someone** — 对某人妄加猜测
- **make room for something** — 为某事留出空间、接纳某事
- **tell the truth** — 说出真相

### 建议练习

1. 先看中文，确认人物关系和故事顺序。
2. 跟读英文标准版，重点模仿停顿和连接方式，不要逐句翻译。
3. 只看 Cue Version 完整复述；卡住时先绕开表达，不立刻查看原文。
4. 复述结束后对照标准版，只修改影响表达清晰度的问题。
