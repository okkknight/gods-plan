# S01E07 — En Garde

> 用途：中文理解剧情 → 学习 Spoken English 标准版 → 使用 Cue Version 脱稿复述  
> 英文长度：约 **288 词**；正常学习语速约 **2.0–2.4 分钟**

---

## 1. 中文剧情描述

这一集的核心是曼尼在击剑方面表现出惊人的天赋。杰伊非常兴奋，把他当成家里的体育明星，并安排他参加比赛。决赛时，曼尼的对手是一位身世可怜、身体又不太方便的小女孩。场边的大人不断给曼尼各种矛盾的暗示：有人希望他全力以赴，有人又觉得应该让着对方。曼尼最后还是赢了比赛，而且庆祝得十分投入，让场面变得更加尴尬。

杰伊对曼尼的骄傲也勾起了米切尔小时候的回忆。米切尔一直觉得父亲更重视克莱尔的花样滑冰，而没有真正支持自己。克莱尔认为事情并不是这样，两人因此争吵。后来，他们重新穿上冰鞋，一边回忆童年，一边意识到当年其实都在用自己的方式支持对方，兄妹关系也因此缓和。

另一边，菲尔开始担心卢克没有任何明显的特长。他让卢克尝试棒球，但卢克表现得很差。菲尔几乎准备放弃时，却发现卢克在自己举办的开放看房活动中非常会和陌生人交流，还能自然地介绍房子。菲尔终于意识到，孩子的才能未必出现在父母提前设想好的地方。

这一集谈的是父母如何看待孩子的天赋。真正重要的不是把孩子塑造成理想中的样子，而是有耐心发现他本来就擅长什么。

## 2. Spoken English 标准版

I’d like to introduce Episode 7 of *Modern Family* Season 1. It’s called **“En Garde.”** The main story begins when the family discovers that Manny is unusually talented at fencing.

Jay is delighted because he finally has a young athlete in the family. He becomes Manny’s biggest supporter and takes the competition very seriously. In the final match, however, Manny has to fence against a girl who has a tragic background and a physical disability. The adults on the side do not know what they want him to do. Some signal that he should compete honestly, while others suggest that he should let her win. Manny ignores the confusion, wins the match, and celebrates very proudly, which makes the situation even more uncomfortable.

Jay’s excitement about Manny also brings back an old argument between Mitchell and Claire. Mitchell believes Jay cared more about Claire’s childhood figure-skating career and never really supported him. Claire remembers it differently. The two siblings start blaming each other, but they eventually put on their skates again and talk about the past. They realize that, in their own imperfect way, they were always there for one another.

Meanwhile, Phil worries that Luke does not have a special talent. He tries to teach him baseball, but Luke is terrible at it. Later, during one of Phil’s open houses, Luke begins talking to potential buyers. He is friendly, confident, and surprisingly good at presenting the house. Phil realizes that Luke does have a natural ability; it is simply not the one Phil was looking for.

Overall, this episode is about recognizing talent without forcing it. Parents often look for the ability they want their child to have, while the real strength may be somewhere completely different.

## 4. 本集可迁移口语表达

- **take something seriously** — 认真对待某事
- **send conflicting signals** — 发出互相矛盾的信号
- **bring back an old argument** — 重新勾起旧争执
- **be there for someone** — 在某人需要时支持他
- **be a natural at something** — 天生擅长某事

### 建议练习

1. 先看中文，确认人物关系和故事顺序。
2. 跟读英文标准版，重点模仿停顿和连接方式，不要逐句翻译。
3. 只看 Cue Version 完整复述；卡住时先绕开表达，不立刻查看原文。
4. 复述结束后对照标准版，只修改影响表达清晰度的问题。
