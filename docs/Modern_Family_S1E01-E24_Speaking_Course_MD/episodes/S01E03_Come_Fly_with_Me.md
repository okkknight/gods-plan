# S01E03 — Come Fly with Me

## 1. 中文剧情描述

这一集主要讲的是几位原本不太亲近的家庭成员，在相处中慢慢了解和接受彼此。

第一条故事线是菲尔和杰伊。菲尔一直很想得到岳父杰伊的认可，于是主动陪他去玩新买的遥控飞机。杰伊本来只想安静地享受自己的爱好，但菲尔一路兴奋地说个不停，让他越来越不耐烦。后来，杰伊让菲尔拿着一个圆环站在远处，说要让飞机从中间穿过去，结果飞机却直接撞到了菲尔的脸上。杰伊坚称这只是意外，但克莱尔怀疑他是故意的，并要求他道歉。最后，杰伊承认菲尔有时确实很烦，不过自己并不讨厌他。对菲尔来说，这已经算是一种难得的肯定。

第二条故事线是格洛丽亚和亚历克斯。克莱尔想让亚历克斯参加婚礼时穿裙子，格洛丽亚便带她去买衣服。亚历克斯觉得海莉才是家里漂亮又受欢迎的女孩，而自己只是那个聪明的孩子。格洛丽亚没有强迫她，而是告诉她不必和姐姐比较，并帮助她看到自己的魅力。与此同时，曼尼和克莱尔聊天，提醒她不要总想把亚历克斯变成自己期待的样子。

第三条故事线是米切尔和卡梅隆。卡梅隆带米切尔去 Costco 买尿布。米切尔起初很看不起这种仓储式超市，可一看到便宜的价格和成箱的商品，他马上改变了态度，最后反而买得最多。

这一集的共同主题是：人很容易先入为主，但真正相处以后，往往会看到对方意想不到的一面。

## 2. Spoken English 标准版

I’d like to introduce Episode 3 of *Modern Family* Season 1. It’s called **“Come Fly with Me.”** This episode has three main storylines, and they are all about family members getting to know one another better.

The first story is about Phil and Jay. Phil really wants a closer relationship with his father-in-law, but Jay usually keeps him at a distance. One day, Phil finds out that Jay is going to fly his new model airplane, so he invites himself along. Phil is extremely excited and keeps talking, while Jay just wants to enjoy his hobby in peace. After a while, Jay asks Phil to stand far away and hold up a large hoop. He says he is going to fly the plane through it, but the plane hits Phil right in the face instead. Jay claims it was an accident, although Claire is pretty sure he did it on purpose. She makes Jay apologize. In the end, Jay admits that Phil can get on his nerves, but he does not actually dislike him. Phil is thrilled because he has always wanted Jay’s approval.

The second story is about Gloria and Alex. Claire wants Alex to wear a dress to a family wedding, so Gloria takes her shopping. Alex feels uncomfortable because she thinks Haley is the pretty and popular sister, while she is only known for being smart. Gloria tells her not to compare herself with Haley and helps her see that she is attractive in her own way. Meanwhile, Manny gives Claire some surprisingly mature advice. He tells her that she should accept Alex instead of trying to turn her into someone else.

The third story is about Mitchell and Cameron. Cameron takes Mitchell to Costco to buy diapers. Mitchell looks down on the store at first, but once he sees the low prices and the huge selection, he completely changes his mind. By the end, the person who did not want to go is the one filling several shopping carts.

Overall, the episode shows that people are often different from our first impression of them. It is funny, but it also moves several family relationships forward.

## 4. 本集可迁移口语表达

- **want someone’s approval** — 希望得到某人的认可
- **get on someone’s nerves** — 让某人心烦
- **look down on something** — 看不起某事物
- **change one’s mind** — 改变主意
- **accept someone for who they are** — 接受真实的某人
