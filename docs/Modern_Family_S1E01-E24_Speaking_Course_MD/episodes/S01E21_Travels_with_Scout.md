# S01E21 — Travels with Scout

> 用途：中文理解剧情 → 学习 Spoken English 标准版 → 使用 Cue Version 脱稿复述  
> 英文长度：约 **300 词**；正常学习语速约 **2.1–2.5 分钟**

---

## 1. 中文剧情描述

这一集里，菲尔的父亲弗兰克开着房车突然来访，还带来一只名叫 Scout 的狗。弗兰克说自己暂时无法照顾它，希望菲尔一家收养。克莱尔一开始坚决反对，认为家里已经够乱了，但相处几天后，她反而成了最依恋 Scout 的人。弗兰克准备离开时又决定把狗带走，克莱尔表面上装作不在意，实际上非常难过。

杰伊遇到米切尔以前的演员朋友本，而本刚出演了一部恐怖电影。杰伊不知道电影类型，带曼尼去看，结果曼尼被吓得不敢独自睡觉。为了消除他的恐惧，杰伊邀请本本人来家里，让曼尼看到演员并不可怕。偏偏门铃坏了，本只能拿着电影道具从窗外突然出现，反而把曼尼吓得更严重。

另一边，迪伦的乐队缺少鼓手。卡梅隆说自己以前会打鼓，并参加试奏。他一开始表现得很差，换了一种握鼓棒的方式后却突然展现出真正的水平。米切尔原本不希望他参加演出，后来亲眼看到卡梅隆在舞台上的状态，也为他感到骄傲。然而，原来的鼓手很快又回来了，卡梅隆的短暂摇滚生涯随即结束。

这一集讲的是短暂进入生活的人和事。狗、演员和乐队都只停留了一会儿，却让角色们看到自己新的情感和能力。

## 2. Spoken English 标准版

I’d like to introduce Episode 21 of *Modern Family* Season 1. It’s called **“Travels with Scout.”** Several temporary visitors enter the family’s life and create unexpected emotional reactions.

Phil’s father, Frank, arrives in his motor home with a dog named Scout. He says he cannot take care of the dog for a while and asks the Dunphys to keep him. Claire strongly objects because she believes the house is already chaotic enough. However, after spending time with Scout, she becomes the person most attached to him. When Frank decides to take the dog with him again, Claire pretends not to care, but she is clearly heartbroken.

Meanwhile, Jay meets Ben, one of Mitchell’s old acting friends, who has appeared in a new horror movie. Jay does not realize it is a horror film and takes Manny to see it. Manny becomes terrified and starts sleeping in Jay and Gloria’s bed. Jay invites Ben to the house to show Manny that the actor is just a normal person. Unfortunately, the doorbell is broken, so Ben appears outside the window while holding a fake machete from the movie. Instead of curing Manny’s fear, the visit makes it much worse.

In the third storyline, Dylan’s band needs a new drummer. Cameron says he used to play and auditions for the group. His first attempt is poor, but after switching the drumsticks to the correct hands, he becomes impressive. Mitchell is annoyed at first, then attends the concert and feels proud of Cameron. The original drummer soon returns, so Cameron’s career in the band ends almost immediately.

Overall, the episode shows that even a short experience can reveal a new side of someone. Claire discovers how attached she can become, and Cameron gets to be a performer again, even if only for one night.

## 4. 本集可迁移口语表达

- **be attached to someone or something** — 对某人或某物产生依恋
- **pretend not to care** — 假装不在意
- **make a fear worse** — 让恐惧加重
- **audition for a group** — 参加团体试演
- **reveal a new side of someone** — 展现某人的新一面

### 建议练习

1. 先看中文，确认人物关系和故事顺序。
2. 跟读英文标准版，重点模仿停顿和连接方式，不要逐句翻译。
3. 只看 Cue Version 完整复述；卡住时先绕开表达，不立刻查看原文。
4. 复述结束后对照标准版，只修改影响表达清晰度的问题。
