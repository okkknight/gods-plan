## 1. 中文剧情描述

这一集延续上一集，全家抵达夏威夷。菲尔想把旅行变成自己和克莱尔从未真正拥有过的蜜月，因为当年克莱尔怀上海莉，两人的婚礼和蜜月都很仓促。他努力安排二人世界，但孩子们不断出现。海莉和刚认识的年轻人出去玩后喝醉了，克莱尔一边照顾她，一边严肃地告诉她酒精带来的后果。后来，菲尔为克莱尔准备了一场简单的婚礼仪式，让他们重新说出誓言，弥补多年前缺少的浪漫。

杰伊原本只想在度假时吃东西、晒太阳，却接到哥哥的电话，想起父亲正是在六十三岁时因健康问题去世，而自己也刚满六十三岁。他突然开始疯狂锻炼，拒绝放松，最后把背弄伤。杰伊终于向格洛丽亚承认，自己其实是害怕死亡。说出来以后，他才愿意真正停下来享受假期。

米切尔想充分利用旅行去参观各个景点，卡梅隆只想躺在酒店休息。两个人先是勉强迁就对方，后来决定各做各的，关系反而轻松了。不过，他们在争论和游玩中两次把莉莉忘在不同地方，幸好最后都找了回来。

这一集讲的是度假并不会自动让人放松。人仍然会带着焦虑、责任和期待出发。真正的休息，往往要等到人愿意承认自己在担心什么。

## 2. Spoken English 标准版

I’d like to introduce Episode 23 of *Modern Family* Season 1. It’s called **“Hawaii,”** and it continues the family vacation that began in the previous episode.

Phil wants to turn the trip into the honeymoon that he and Claire never really had. Claire became pregnant with Haley before their wedding, so the ceremony and honeymoon were both rushed. Phil tries to create private romantic time, but their children keep interrupting. Haley goes out with some young people she has just met and becomes drunk. Claire takes care of her and warns her that alcohol will always have consequences. Later, Phil surprises Claire with a small wedding ceremony, giving them a chance to renew their vows and experience some of the romance they missed years earlier.

Jay plans to spend the vacation eating and relaxing. Then a call from his brother reminds him that their father died at the age of sixty-three because of poor health. Jay has just turned sixty-three himself, so he suddenly becomes obsessed with exercise. He works out instead of enjoying the trip and eventually hurts his back. Jay finally admits to Gloria that he is afraid of dying. Once he says it aloud, he is able to slow down and enjoy the vacation with her.

Meanwhile, Mitchell wants to visit every attraction, while Cameron wants to relax at the hotel. They try to force each other to follow the same schedule before realizing they can do separate things. Unfortunately, they also manage to leave Lily behind twice, once near an elevator and later during another outing.

Overall, the episode shows that travel does not automatically remove anxiety. People bring their fears and expectations with them, and they can only relax after they acknowledge what is really bothering them.

## 4. 本集可迁移口语表达

- **turn a trip into a honeymoon** — 把旅行变成蜜月
- **renew one’s vows** — 重温婚姻誓言
- **become obsessed with something** — 变得沉迷于某事
- **say something aloud** — 把某事说出口
- **acknowledge what is bothering someone** — 承认真正困扰自己的事情

### 建议练习

1. 先看中文，确认人物关系和故事顺序。
2. 跟读英文标准版，重点模仿停顿和连接方式，不要逐句翻译。
3. 只看 Cue Version 完整复述；卡住时先绕开表达，不立刻查看原文。
4. 复述结束后对照标准版，只修改影响表达清晰度的问题。
