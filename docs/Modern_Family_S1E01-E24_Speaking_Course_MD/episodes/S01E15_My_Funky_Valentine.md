# S01E15 — My Funky Valentine

> 用途：中文理解剧情 → 学习 Spoken English 标准版 → 使用 Cue Version 脱稿复述  
> 英文长度：约 **280 词**；正常学习语速约 **1.9–2.3 分钟**

---

## 1. 中文剧情描述

这一集发生在情人节。菲尔和克莱尔想摆脱日常的父母身份，像陌生人一样在酒店酒吧里角色扮演。菲尔化名克莱夫，克莱尔化名朱莉安娜。开始时两人都很投入，但克莱尔的外套被自动扶梯夹住，她又没有穿完整的衣服，导致两人不得不在公共场合想尽办法脱身。原本浪漫刺激的计划最后变成一场非常尴尬的逃生行动。格洛丽亚碰巧出现，帮他们保住了一点体面。

米切尔原本准备在法庭上发表一段精彩的结案陈词，案件却在最后一刻和解，让他失去了展示机会。与此同时，曼尼写给喜欢的女孩的一首诗被另一个男孩冒领。米切尔于是把自己没能在法庭上说出的激情，用来替曼尼“辩护”。他的发言很有气势，也揭穿了真相，可女孩最后还是选择了另一个男孩，让这场胜利显得有些空洞。

杰伊带格洛丽亚去看单口喜剧，希望度过一个轻松的夜晚。喜剧演员却不断拿他们巨大的年龄差开玩笑。杰伊越来越不舒服，最后和格洛丽亚离开，去跳他们更喜欢的萨尔萨舞。途中，他们还帮陷入困境的菲尔和克莱尔收场。

这一集说明，浪漫很少按照计划出现。人可以精心安排角色、地点和台词，但真正亲密的时刻，往往来自两个人一起面对尴尬。

## 2. Spoken English 标准版

I’d like to introduce Episode 15 of *Modern Family* Season 1. It’s called **“My Funky Valentine,”** and it follows the family on Valentine’s Day.

Phil and Claire want to escape their usual roles as parents, so they meet at a hotel bar and pretend to be strangers. Phil becomes Clive, and Claire becomes Juliana. The role-play is exciting at first, but Claire’s coat gets caught in an escalator. Because she is not fully dressed underneath it, the couple has to find a way to free her without creating a public scene. Their romantic adventure turns into an embarrassing rescue operation. Gloria happens to be nearby and helps them escape with at least some dignity left.

Meanwhile, Mitchell is preparing to give an impressive closing argument in court, but the case settles before he gets the chance. Manny has a problem of his own: another boy takes credit for a poem Manny wrote for a girl. Mitchell uses all the passion from his unused courtroom speech to defend Manny and expose the truth. He wins the argument, but the girl still chooses the other boy, so the victory does not feel very satisfying.

Jay takes Gloria to a comedy show for Valentine’s Day. The comedian keeps making jokes about their age difference, and Jay becomes increasingly uncomfortable. They leave and go salsa dancing instead, choosing something they both actually enjoy. On the way, they also help Phil and Claire out of their difficult situation.

Overall, the episode shows that romance rarely follows a perfect script. The best moments do not come from acting sophisticated. They come from being able to survive an embarrassing situation together and still laugh about it later.

## 4. 本集可迁移口语表达

- **pretend to be strangers** — 假装成陌生人
- **get caught in something** — 被某物夹住或缠住
- **take credit for something** — 把别人的功劳算在自己头上
- **expose the truth** — 揭露真相
- **with some dignity left** — 还保留一点体面

### 建议练习

1. 先看中文，确认人物关系和故事顺序。
2. 跟读英文标准版，重点模仿停顿和连接方式，不要逐句翻译。
3. 只看 Cue Version 完整复述；卡住时先绕开表达，不立刻查看原文。
4. 复述结束后对照标准版，只修改影响表达清晰度的问题。
