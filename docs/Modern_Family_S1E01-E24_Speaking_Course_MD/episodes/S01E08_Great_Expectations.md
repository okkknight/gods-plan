# S01E08 — Great Expectations

> 用途：中文理解剧情 → 学习 Spoken English 标准版 → 使用 Cue Version 脱稿复述  
> 英文长度：约 **289 词**；正常学习语速约 **2.0–2.4 分钟**

## 1. 中文剧情描述

这一集发生在菲尔和克莱尔的结婚纪念日。两个人有一个传统：每年都要给对方准备一份充满心意的礼物。菲尔总能送出非常用心的东西，克莱尔因此压力很大。今年，她请来了菲尔年轻时非常喜欢的音乐人伊兹·拉方丹，在家里为他表演。问题是，克莱尔把菲尔所说的“我们的歌”记错了。那首歌其实和菲尔的一段尴尬往事有关，导致浪漫惊喜一度变成灾难。好在菲尔最终理解了她的心意，歌手也临时写了一首新歌，让纪念日重新变得温暖。

与此同时，杰伊安排了一次“杰伊之夜”，让孙辈们到家里过夜。他准备了电影、食物和各种活动，但海莉只想去参加朋友的派对。令人意外的是，迪伦反而很享受家庭活动。亚历克斯还故意骗卢克说杰伊快要死了，卢克因此一直格外认真地陪着外公。

另一边，米切尔和卡梅隆想暂时摆脱育儿生活，和老朋友萨尔出去玩。他们很快发现，萨尔对莉莉充满嫉妒，因为孩子改变了他们三个人过去的相处方式。最终，几个人也来到杰伊家，所有故事线汇到了一起。

这一集讲的是“期待”与现实之间的差距。精心安排的夜晚总会出现意外，但真正重要的并不是计划是否完美，而是人们是否愿意留下来陪伴彼此。

## 2. Spoken English 标准版

I’d like to introduce Episode 8 of *Modern Family* Season 1. It’s called **“Great Expectations.”** The title fits the episode because several characters plan a perfect evening, but reality does not follow the plan.

The first storyline is about Phil and Claire’s wedding anniversary. Every year, they try to give each other a meaningful gift. Phil is very good at this, so Claire feels pressure to come up with something equally special. This year, she hires Izzy LaFontaine, a musician Phil loved when he was younger, to perform in their home. Claire also asks him to play what she believes is “their song.” Unfortunately, she has remembered the story incorrectly. The song is connected to an embarrassing memory from Phil’s past, and the romantic surprise becomes extremely awkward. Phil still appreciates the effort, and the musician creates a new song for them, so the evening ends on a warm note.

Meanwhile, Jay hosts “Jay’s Night,” a sleepover for his grandchildren. He plans movies, food, and family activities. Haley wants to leave for a party, but Dylan unexpectedly enjoys spending time with Jay. Alex tells Luke that Jay may not have long to live, so Luke becomes unusually attentive and treats every moment as important.

The third story is about Mitchell and Cameron. They go out with their old friend Sal, hoping to feel like their pre-parenthood selves again. However, Sal is jealous of Lily because the baby has changed their friendship. Their night does not go as planned, and they eventually end up at Jay’s house with everyone else.

Overall, the episode shows that a perfect plan is not what makes an evening meaningful. The plans fall apart, but the family still finds a way to enjoy being together.

## 4. 本集可迁移口语表达

- **come up with something** — 想出某事
- **remember something incorrectly** — 把某事记错
- **end on a warm note** — 以温暖的方式收尾
- **not go as planned** — 没有按计划进行
- **feel like one’s old self** — 找回从前的自己

### 建议练习

1. 先看中文，确认人物关系和故事顺序。
2. 跟读英文标准版，重点模仿停顿和连接方式，不要逐句翻译。
3. 只看 Cue Version 完整复述；卡住时先绕开表达，不立刻查看原文。
4. 复述结束后对照标准版，只修改影响表达清晰度的问题。
