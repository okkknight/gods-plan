# S01E14 — Moon Landing

## 1. 中文剧情描述

这一集里，格洛丽亚发生了一场小车祸。她坚称完全是对方的错，还让当律师的米切尔帮自己处理。曼尼却直接指出，格洛丽亚平时开车本来就很危险。格洛丽亚觉得家人不相信自己，十分生气。后来她又把车开进一家餐馆，终于不得不承认自己的驾驶技术可能真的有问题，并再次向米切尔求助。

克莱尔遇到过去的同事瓦莱丽。瓦莱丽事业成功、生活精致，让已经离开职场多年的克莱尔觉得自己似乎没有取得什么成就。为了证明自己的家庭生活同样值得骄傲，克莱尔邀请她到家里吃饭，希望孩子们表现得完美。结果一家人从头到尾都在制造混乱。克莱尔一度非常难堪，但最后也看到瓦莱丽的生活并不像表面上那么圆满。

另一边，杰伊和卡梅隆一起打壁球。赛前在更衣室里，两个人弯腰时不小心发生了一次尴尬的身体接触。杰伊一直无法把这件事从脑子里赶走，比赛时完全不能集中注意力。卡梅隆反而表现得很自然，让杰伊显得更加窘迫。

这一集讲的是自尊心如何让人拒绝承认尴尬和不足。格洛丽亚不愿承认自己开车差，克莱尔不愿承认自己羡慕旧同事，杰伊则无法自然面对一次无意的身体接触。越想掩饰，事情反而越明显。

## 2. Spoken English 标准版

I’d like to introduce Episode 14 of *Modern Family* Season 1. It’s called **“Moon Landing.”** The episode is mainly about embarrassment and the different ways people try to protect their pride.

Gloria gets into a minor car accident and insists that the other driver was completely at fault. She asks Mitchell, who is a lawyer, to help her. Manny honestly points out that Gloria is not a very safe driver, but she becomes angry because she feels that her own family does not believe her. Later, she drives into a restaurant. At that point, she has to admit that her driving may be part of the problem, and she asks Mitchell for legal help again.

Meanwhile, Claire runs into Valerie, a successful former coworker. Valerie’s career makes Claire feel insecure about leaving the professional world to raise a family. Claire invites her home because she wants to prove that her life is just as impressive. She hopes the children and Phil will behave perfectly, but the entire visit becomes chaotic. Claire is embarrassed, although she also begins to see that Valerie’s apparently perfect life is not as complete as it looks.

The third story is about Jay and Cameron playing racquetball. In the locker room, they accidentally touch while both bending over. The moment is completely innocent, but Jay cannot stop thinking about it. During the game, he becomes so uncomfortable that he cannot concentrate. Cameron treats it as no big deal, which only makes Jay feel more awkward.

Overall, the episode shows that embarrassment becomes more powerful when people refuse to acknowledge it. Gloria, Claire, and Jay all try to hide an uncomfortable truth, but their efforts only make it more obvious.

## 4. 本集可迁移口语表达

- **be at fault** — 负有责任
- **feel insecure about something** — 对某事感到不自信
- **run into someone** — 偶然遇到某人
- **be no big deal** — 没什么大不了
- **refuse to acknowledge something** — 拒绝承认某事

### 建议练习

1. 先看中文，确认人物关系和故事顺序。
2. 跟读英文标准版，重点模仿停顿和连接方式，不要逐句翻译。
3. 只看 Cue Version 完整复述；卡住时先绕开表达，不立刻查看原文。
4. 复述结束后对照标准版，只修改影响表达清晰度的问题。
