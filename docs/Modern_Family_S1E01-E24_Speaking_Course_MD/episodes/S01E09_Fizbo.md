# S01E09 — Fizbo

> 用途：中文理解剧情 → 学习 Spoken English 标准版 → 使用 Cue Version 脱稿复述

## 1. 中文剧情描述

这一集围绕卢克的生日派对展开。菲尔和克莱尔对派对有完全不同的想法：克莱尔想控制风险，只准备简单的手工活动；菲尔则认为生日一定要盛大，于是找来了充气城堡、动物表演、攀岩墙和各种娱乐项目。看似热闹的安排互相影响，最终引发一连串事故，卢克也摔断了手臂。

卡梅隆决定扮成自己最喜欢的小丑“菲兹波”参加派对。米切尔觉得这个形象太夸张，也不想别人看到卡梅隆这样打扮。但当一个加油站的男人嘲笑米切尔时，穿着小丑服的卡梅隆立刻站出来保护他。米切尔因此看到，这个荒唐的角色其实也代表了卡梅隆自信、勇敢的一面。与此同时，菲尔一直隐瞒自己害怕小丑，但面对菲兹波时，他的恐惧完全暴露了出来。

曼尼想在派对上吸引同学比安卡的注意，于是照着杰伊的建议假装冷淡。这个策略没有什么效果，不过在混乱中，比安卡遇到危险，曼尼勇敢地帮助了她。海莉则嫉妒动物管理员受到迪伦关注，冲动之下放出了一只蝎子，使整个派对更加失控。

虽然生日派对几乎变成一场灾难，卢克最后却说这是自己最棒的生日。对孩子来说，受伤和混乱并没有毁掉这一天，反而让它成为永远不会忘记的回忆。

## 2. Spoken English 标准版

I’d like to introduce Episode 9 of *Modern Family* Season 1. It’s called **“Fizbo,”** and it takes place during Luke’s birthday party.

Claire and Phil have completely different ideas about the party. Claire wants everything to be safe and controlled, so she prepares a simple craft table. Phil believes a birthday should be exciting, so he adds a bounce house, a rock-climbing wall, an animal show, and several other attractions. Each activity seems harmless by itself, but together they create a chain reaction of accidents. In the end, Luke falls and breaks his arm.

Cameron arrives dressed as Fizbo, the clown character he has performed as for years. Mitchell is embarrassed by the costume and wishes Cameron would act more normally. Later, a man at a gas station makes fun of Mitchell. Cameron, still dressed as Fizbo, confidently stands up for him. Mitchell realizes that the ridiculous costume also brings out Cameron’s brave and protective side. Phil has his own problem with Fizbo because he is secretly terrified of clowns, and he can no longer hide it.

Meanwhile, Manny wants to impress a girl named Bianca. Jay tells him to act less interested, but the strategy does not work very well. During the chaos, Manny gets a chance to help Bianca when she is in danger, and his real courage is more effective than any dating trick. Haley also becomes jealous when Dylan pays attention to the animal handler, so she releases a scorpion and makes the party even more dangerous.

Although the party is a disaster from the adults’ point of view, Luke says it was the best birthday he has ever had. The episode shows that children and adults can experience the same event in completely different ways.

## 4. 本集可迁移口语表达

- **create a chain reaction** — 引起连锁反应
- **stand up for someone** — 挺身维护某人
- **bring out someone’s ... side** — 展现某人的某一面
- **act less interested** — 表现得没那么感兴趣
- **from someone’s point of view** — 从某人的角度看

### 建议练习

1. 先看中文，确认人物关系和故事顺序。
2. 跟读英文标准版，重点模仿停顿和连接方式，不要逐句翻译。
3. 只看 Cue Version 完整复述；卡住时先绕开表达，不立刻查看原文。
4. 复述结束后对照标准版，只修改影响表达清晰度的问题。
