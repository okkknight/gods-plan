## 1. 中文剧情描述

第一集主要介绍《摩登家庭》中的三个家庭。刚开始时，他们看起来彼此独立，直到结尾我们才知道，原来所有人都属于同一个大家庭。

第一条故事线是克莱尔和菲尔一家。克莱尔和菲尔有三个孩子：海莉、亚历克斯和卢克。海莉第一次把男朋友迪伦带回家，克莱尔非常紧张，总担心他们在楼上做出越界的事。菲尔则一直强调自己是一个懂年轻人的“酷爸爸”，还想吓唬迪伦，结果反而闪到了自己的腰。与此同时，卢克用玩具枪打中了亚历克斯。按照家里之前定下的规矩，菲尔必须用同一把枪打卢克一下，让他承担后果。菲尔一直拖延，最后却意外打中了卢克、迪伦和自己。

第二条故事线是杰伊、格洛丽亚和曼尼。杰伊比格洛丽亚年长很多，因此常被别人误认为是她的父亲，这让他有些不自在。格洛丽亚的儿子曼尼早熟、浪漫，还喜欢上了一个十六岁的女孩。他准备带着鲜花和诗去表白。杰伊觉得他只会让自己难堪，格洛丽亚却认为家人应该支持他的勇气。女孩拒绝曼尼后，杰伊也开始意识到，自己现在是曼尼的家人，不能只站在旁边批评他。

第三条故事线是米切尔和卡梅隆。他们从越南收养了一个女婴莉莉。在回程的飞机上，米切尔误以为其他乘客在歧视他们，激动地发表了一番演说，后来才发现自己误会了。回家后，他们准备把莉莉介绍给家人。杰伊起初怀疑他们是否适合当父亲，但当卡梅隆伴着《狮子王》的音乐戏剧性地抱出莉莉时，整个家庭都接受并欢迎了她。

这一集最后揭示，杰伊是克莱尔和米切尔的父亲。三个看起来完全不同的家庭，其实共同组成了这个吵闹、尴尬但彼此支持的大家庭。

## 2. Spoken English 标准版

I’d like to introduce the first episode of *Modern Family*. It is called **“Pilot.”** This episode introduces three very different families. At first, they seem separate, but in the end, we discover that they are all connected.

The first family is the Dunphy family. Claire and Phil have three children: Haley, Alex, and Luke. Haley invites her older boyfriend, Dylan, to their house for the first time. Claire becomes very nervous and keeps checking on them because she is afraid they may do something inappropriate upstairs. Phil, on the other hand, wants to be the “cool dad.” He tries to act relaxed and even plans to scare Dylan a little, but he only hurts his back and embarrasses himself.

At the same time, Luke has shot Alex with a toy BB gun. Claire reminds Phil that they made a rule: if Luke shoots someone, Phil has to shoot him back so that he learns a lesson. Phil keeps putting it off because he does not really want to hurt Luke. In the end, the gun goes off by accident, and Phil shoots Luke, Dylan, and then himself.

The second family is Jay, Gloria, and Manny. Jay is much older than Gloria, and people sometimes mistake him for her father. This makes him feel insecure about his age. Manny, Gloria’s eleven-year-old son, is romantic and unusually mature. He has a crush on a sixteen-year-old girl and decides to read her a poem. Jay thinks Manny will only make a fool of himself, but Gloria believes the family should support him. The girl tells Manny that she already has a boyfriend, and he is heartbroken. Jay slowly begins to understand that being part of Manny’s family means standing behind him, even when he expects him to fail.

The third family is Mitchell and Cameron. They are returning from Vietnam with their newly adopted daughter, Lily. On the plane, Mitchell thinks the other passengers are judging them, so he gives an angry speech. He soon realizes that he completely misunderstood the situation. Later, Mitchell and Cameron introduce Lily to the whole family. Jay is doubtful at first, but Cameron makes a dramatic entrance while holding Lily in the air. Everyone immediately welcomes her.

The final scene reveals that Jay is Claire and Mitchell’s father. Overall, the episode shows us a family that is complicated, awkward, and sometimes ridiculous, but also loving and supportive.

## 4. 本集可迁移口语表达

- **introduce three different families** — 介绍三个不同的家庭
- **keep checking on someone** — 不断查看某人的情况
- **follow through** — 把已经决定的事执行到底
- **make a fool of oneself** — 出丑、让自己显得可笑
- **stand behind someone** — 支持某人、做某人的后盾

### 建议练习

1. 第一遍只练“三个家庭分别是谁”，不要急着讲全部细节。
2. 第二遍在每个家庭下补上一个主要冲突和一个结尾。
3. 最后一遍完整复述，并在结尾说清楚三家人的关系。
4. 这集人物很多，允许用简单句；人物关系清楚比句式复杂更重要。
