# S01E12 — Not in My House

> 用途：中文理解剧情 → 学习 Spoken English 标准版 → 使用 Cue Version 脱稿复述  
> 英文长度：约 **272 词**；正常学习语速约 **1.9–2.3 分钟**

---

## 1. 中文剧情描述

这一集从克莱尔在电脑上发现一张色情图片开始。她认定这是卢克下载的，于是要求菲尔处理。菲尔其实知道图片是自己的，却不敢承认，只能假装严厉地教育卢克。与此同时，卢克又因为偷看海莉的日记而真正犯了错。两件事混在一起后，每个人都以为对方在说另一件事，形成了一场充满误会的“坦白”。最后，菲尔不得不承认图片属于自己，也失去了继续教训卢克的资格。

杰伊买回一个名叫 Barkley 的狗形管家雕像，觉得它非常有趣，格洛丽亚却认为它又丑又吓人。两人为这件毫无意义的小摆设争执不休。杰伊最后把它送给米切尔和卡梅隆，没想到卡梅隆非常喜欢，米切尔则和格洛丽亚一样无法接受。

米切尔和卡梅隆还注意到自家的园丁总显得很悲伤。他们听不懂西班牙语，便让格洛丽亚帮忙翻译，却误以为园丁正经历严重的家庭问题。后来他们才知道，他只是因为婚礼安排感到压力。两人热心地把家借给他举行婚礼，结果再次发现，帮助别人之前最好先弄清楚对方到底需要什么。

这一集讲的是误会如何在隐瞒和想当然中不断扩大。很多冲突并不是因为问题本身有多严重，而是因为大家都只听见了自己以为对方在说的内容。

## 2. Spoken English 标准版

I’d like to introduce Episode 12 of *Modern Family* Season 1. It’s called **“Not in My House.”** Most of the comedy comes from people hiding the truth or misunderstanding what someone else is talking about.

Claire finds an inappropriate picture on the family computer and assumes Luke downloaded it. She tells Phil to deal with him. Phil actually knows the picture is his, but he is too embarrassed to admit it, so he pretends to give Luke a serious lecture. At the same time, Luke really has done something wrong: he read Haley’s private journal. When the family starts discussing both problems, everyone thinks they are talking about the same thing. Luke confesses to reading the journal, while Claire believes he is admitting to the picture. The confusion grows until Phil finally has to tell the truth and admit that the picture was his.

At Jay’s house, Jay buys a strange dog-shaped butler statue named Barkley. He thinks it is funny and charming, but Gloria finds it ugly and disturbing. They argue over an object that should not matter at all. Jay eventually gives Barkley to Mitchell and Cameron. Cameron loves it immediately, while Mitchell reacts almost exactly like Gloria.

Mitchell and Cameron also become concerned about their gardener, who always looks sad. Because they do not understand his Spanish, they imagine that he has a serious personal problem. They later learn that he is simply stressed about his wedding. Wanting to help, they offer their house for the ceremony.

Overall, the episode shows how quickly confusion grows when people hide information and fill in the missing details with their own assumptions.

## 4. 本集可迁移口语表达

- **deal with a problem** — 处理问题
- **be too embarrassed to admit something** — 尴尬得不敢承认某事
- **fill in the missing details** — 自行补全缺失的信息
- **offer one’s house for something** — 把家借给别人做某事
- **tell the truth** — 坦白事实

### 建议练习

1. 先看中文，确认人物关系和故事顺序。
2. 跟读英文标准版，重点模仿停顿和连接方式，不要逐句翻译。
3. 只看 Cue Version 完整复述；卡住时先绕开表达，不立刻查看原文。
4. 复述结束后对照标准版，只修改影响表达清晰度的问题。
