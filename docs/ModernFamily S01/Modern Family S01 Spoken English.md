# Modern Family S01E01 — Pilot

> 463 words

This episode introduces three families who seem separate at first. Each family has its own problems, but by the end, we find out they’re all connected.

The first family is Claire and Phil Dunphy and their three children, Haley, Alex, and Luke. Claire is the stricter parent, while Phil likes to think he’s a “cool dad.” Their fifteen-year-old daughter Haley invites a senior named Dylan over, and Claire immediately starts worrying. She remembers the mistakes she made as a teenager and doesn’t want Haley to do the same things.

Claire asks Phil to scare Dylan a little, but Phil’s attempt to act cool only embarrasses everyone. Claire keeps checking on Haley upstairs, which leads to an argument between them. At the same time, Phil has another parenting problem. Luke shot Alex with a BB gun, and Phil had promised that if Luke ever shot someone, he would shoot Luke back. Phil tries to carry out the punishment but can’t do it properly. In the confusion, he accidentally shoots Dylan instead.

The second family is Jay, his much younger wife Gloria, and Gloria’s eleven-year-old son, Manny. Jay is already uncomfortable about their age difference, especially after another parent mistakes him for Gloria’s father.

Manny, meanwhile, has fallen for a sixteen-year-old girl named Brenda. He decides to go to the mall and read her a poem. Jay thinks this is a terrible idea and tries to stop him because he doesn’t want Manny to make a fool of himself. Gloria tells Jay that Manny is his family now, so he should support him instead of putting him down.

Manny reads the poem, but Brenda tells him she already has a boyfriend. He comes back disappointed, and Jay finally tells him that what he did was brave.

The third family is Mitchell and his partner Cameron. They’ve just adopted a baby girl named Lily from Vietnam. Mitchell is happy, but he’s nervous about telling his family because he expects his father to judge him. Cameron knows Mitchell will keep avoiding the conversation, so he secretly invites the whole family over for dinner.

At dinner, the three stories finally come together. Jay is Claire and Mitchell’s father, which means all three households are part of the same family. When Mitchell tells everyone about the adoption, Jay reacts badly and says two men shouldn’t raise a child without a mother.

Then Cameron enters, holding Lily in the air while “Circle of Life” plays. The dramatic entrance is funny, but it also changes the mood. Once Jay sees Lily, he softens. He admits that he’s still making mistakes as a father himself and welcomes her into the family.

The episode shows a family that often embarrasses and annoys one another, but still tries to be there when it matters.
