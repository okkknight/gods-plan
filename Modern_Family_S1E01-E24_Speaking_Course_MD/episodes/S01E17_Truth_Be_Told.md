# S01E17 — Truth Be Told

## 1. 中文剧情描述

这一集里，菲尔通过社交网站重新联系上大学时期的朋友丹妮丝。菲尔觉得这只是一次普通的老友重逢，克莱尔却从一开始就怀疑丹妮丝当年喜欢他。丹妮丝来到家里后，果然不断提起两人过去的暧昧经历，还暗示菲尔曾经也对她有意思。菲尔起初完全没有察觉，直到丹妮丝直接向他靠近，他才意识到克莱尔是对的。丹妮丝离开后，菲尔不得不承认，自己对异性的暗示并没有想象中那么敏锐。

与此同时，亚历克斯为了捉弄卢克，告诉他自己在网上查到，卢克其实是被收养的。卢克信以为真，开始重新解释家里所有人的行为。亚历克斯原本只是开玩笑，后来发现弟弟真的受伤，也意识到自己把恶作剧做过了头。

杰伊不小心弄死了曼尼心爱的乌龟。他不愿承认，于是编造了一个越来越复杂的故事，说乌龟是被浣熊袭击的。曼尼其实很快就看出了真相，只是在等杰伊自己坦白。最后，杰伊终于承认错误。

另一边，米切尔决定鼓起勇气对老板说，自己不能总为了工作错过家庭生活。他以为这会让老板尊重自己，结果却直接被解雇。

这一集讲的是“诚实”不仅意味着不撒谎，也意味着看清自己。很多人都觉得自己很聪明、很善良或很有原则，直到现实给了他们一次不太体面的测试。

## 2. Spoken English 标准版

I’d like to introduce Episode 17 of *Modern Family* Season 1. It’s called **“Truth Be Told.”** Each storyline involves someone avoiding a truth that eventually becomes impossible to ignore.

Phil reconnects online with Denise, an old friend from college. He believes she is simply being friendly, but Claire immediately suspects that Denise had feelings for him. When Denise visits the house, she keeps bringing up private memories and hints that there was always something romantic between them. Phil misses every signal until Denise finally makes her interest obvious. He is shocked, while Claire is not surprised at all. After Denise leaves, Phil has to admit that he is not nearly as good at reading women as he thought.

Meanwhile, Alex plays a cruel prank on Luke. She tells him that she found evidence online that he was adopted. Luke believes her and begins reinterpreting every family interaction as proof that he does not belong. Alex meant it as a joke, but she realizes she has gone too far when she sees that Luke is genuinely hurt.

At Jay’s house, Jay accidentally kills Manny’s pet turtle. Instead of admitting it, he creates an elaborate story about a raccoon attack. Manny quickly figures out what really happened, but he waits for Jay to tell the truth on his own. Jay finally confesses.

Mitchell also tries to be honest. He tells his boss that work should not always come before his family. He expects the boss to respect his courage, but he gets fired instead.

Overall, the episode shows that honesty is not always rewarded immediately. Still, avoiding the truth usually creates a much bigger problem than the truth itself.

## 3. Cue Version

### Opening
- every storyline: a truth becomes impossible to ignore

### Phil and Denise
- reconnect online with old college friend
- Phil thinks she is just friendly / Claire suspects romantic interest
- Denise brings up private memories and sends signals
- Phil misses everything until interest becomes obvious
- admits he is bad at reading the situation

### Alex and Luke
- Alex tells Luke he was adopted as a prank
- Luke believes it → reinterprets family behavior
- Alex realizes she has **gone too far**

### Jay and the turtle
- accidentally kills Manny’s pet turtle
- invents raccoon story
- Manny already knows / waits for confession
- Jay finally tells the truth

### Mitchell and work
- tells boss family must come first sometimes
- expects respect → gets fired

### Closing
- honesty may be uncomfortable / avoiding truth creates a bigger problem

## 4. 本集可迁移口语表达

- **reconnect with someone** — 和某人重新取得联系
- **read the situation** — 看懂形势或暗示
- **go too far** — 做得过火
- **figure out what happened** — 弄清发生了什么
- **come before something** — 比某事更重要、排在某事之前

### 建议练习

1. 先看中文，确认人物关系和故事顺序。
2. 跟读英文标准版，重点模仿停顿和连接方式，不要逐句翻译。
3. 只看 Cue Version 完整复述；卡住时先绕开表达，不立刻查看原文。
4. 复述结束后对照标准版，只修改影响表达清晰度的问题。
