# S01E05 — Coal Digger

> 用途：中文理解剧情 → 学习 Spoken English 标准版 → 使用 Cue Version 脱稿复述  
> 英文长度：约 **289 词**；正常学习语速约 **2.0–2.4 分钟**

## 1. 中文剧情描述

这一集从卢克和曼尼在学校打架开始。曼尼坚持说自己从家庭关系上算是卢克的舅舅，卢克觉得这很荒唐，两个人因此发生冲突。学校把家长叫来后，杰伊出面处理，并试图让孩子们接受这个复杂而奇怪的新家庭关系。

周末，全家人在杰伊家看橄榄球。米切尔知道杰伊很喜欢这项运动，于是提前学习了很多专业术语，希望能和父亲多一些共同话题。然而，他说出来的话总是用错地方，反而显得很尴尬。卡梅隆起初似乎对橄榄球毫无兴趣，后来大家才发现，他年轻时不但打过球，而且还是很优秀的队员。米切尔原本准备好的“共同兴趣”完全被卡梅隆抢走了。

更大的冲突发生在克莱尔和格洛丽亚之间。卢克在争吵中把格洛丽亚叫作“淘金女”，格洛丽亚很快意识到，这个词其实来自克莱尔。她因此非常受伤，认为克莱尔一直觉得自己只是为了钱才嫁给杰伊。克莱尔起初试图解释，但最后意识到自己的确带着偏见。为了真诚道歉，她跳进游泳池，格洛丽亚也跟着跳了进去，其他家人最后纷纷加入。

这一集讲的是一个重组家庭如何面对彼此的怀疑和不满。大家并没有通过一场严肃谈话解决所有问题，而是用一场有点荒唐的集体落水，暂时重新站到了一起。

## 2. Spoken English 标准版

I’d like to introduce Episode 5 of *Modern Family* Season 1. It’s called **“Coal Digger.”** The episode begins with a fight between Luke and Manny at school.

Manny says that, technically, he is Luke’s uncle because Gloria is married to Jay. Luke thinks that sounds ridiculous, and the disagreement turns physical. When the family is called to the school, Jay tries to explain that their family relationships may be unusual, but they are still real and need to be respected.

Later, everyone gathers at Jay’s house to watch football. Mitchell wants to connect with his father, so he studies football terms before the game. Unfortunately, he keeps using them at the wrong time, and the whole effort becomes awkward. Cameron acts as if he does not care much about sports, but then the family discovers that he used to be a very good football player. Jay is immediately impressed, while Mitchell feels that Cameron has accidentally stolen the moment he had prepared for himself.

The main conflict is between Claire and Gloria. During the children’s argument, Luke calls Gloria a “gold digger.” Gloria realizes that he must have heard the expression from Claire. She is deeply hurt because it confirms her fear that Claire thinks she only married Jay for his money. Claire first tries to defend herself, but eventually admits that she has been unfair and judgmental.

To prove that her apology is sincere, Claire jumps into the swimming pool with her clothes on. Gloria joins her, and soon the rest of the family jumps in as well.

Overall, this episode is about distrust inside a blended family. Nobody solves everything with a perfect speech, but the family manages to move past the conflict and come together again.

## 4. 本集可迁移口语表达

- **technically speaking** — 严格来说
- **connect with someone** — 和某人建立联系
- **steal someone’s moment** — 抢走某人的风头
- **be judgmental** — 喜欢评判别人
- **move past a conflict** — 放下并越过一场冲突

### 建议练习

1. 先看中文，确认人物关系和故事顺序。
2. 跟读英文标准版，重点模仿停顿和连接方式，不要逐句翻译。
3. 只看 Cue Version 完整复述；卡住时先绕开表达，不立刻查看原文。
4. 复述结束后对照标准版，只修改影响表达清晰度的问题。
