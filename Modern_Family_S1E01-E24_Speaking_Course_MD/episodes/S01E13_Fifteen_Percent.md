# S01E13 — Fifteen Percent

> 用途：中文理解剧情 → 学习 Spoken English 标准版 → 使用 Cue Version 脱稿复述

## 1. 中文剧情描述

这一集从杰伊把卡梅隆介绍成“米切尔的朋友”开始。米切尔认为父亲仍然没有真正接受自己的伴侣，于是故意暗示杰伊的老朋友肖蒂可能是同性恋，想看看杰伊会怎么反应。杰伊虽然很不自然，却努力表现出支持。后来他才发现肖蒂并不是同性恋，只是欠了赌债，不敢告诉妻子。杰伊借钱帮了他。米切尔看到父亲愿意尝试改变，认为即使一个人只改变百分之十五，也已经是进步。

克莱尔则因为不会使用家里复杂的遥控器而被菲尔嘲笑。她不服气，要求菲尔教海莉学会使用，想证明问题不在自己。结果海莉很快就学会了。克莱尔只好偷偷请海莉教自己，又不愿让菲尔知道。

另一边，格洛丽亚替曼尼安排了一次相亲，并认真帮他打扮。女孩惠特尼来到家里后，却把卡梅隆误认为自己的约会对象，而且明显更喜欢成熟、会聊天的卡梅隆。曼尼虽然受挫，但仍努力保持体面。

这一集的主题很直接：人不会突然变成完全不同的人，但一点点改变也值得被看见。无论是杰伊学习接受卡梅隆，还是克莱尔承认自己不会用遥控器，进步往往从放下一点自尊开始。

## 2. Spoken English 标准版

I’d like to introduce Episode 13 of *Modern Family* Season 1. It’s called **“Fifteen Percent.”** The title comes from Mitchell’s idea that people may not change completely, but even a small change can still matter.

The first story begins when Jay introduces Cameron as “Mitchell’s friend” instead of his partner. Mitchell feels that Jay still has not fully accepted their relationship. To test him, Mitchell suggests that Jay’s old friend Shorty might be gay. Jay is uncomfortable, but he tries hard to respond in a supportive way. Later, Jay learns that Shorty is not gay at all. He has a gambling debt and is afraid to tell his wife. Jay quietly lends him the money. Mitchell notices that his father is at least trying to become more open-minded, and he decides that a fifteen-percent improvement is still real progress.

Meanwhile, Claire cannot operate the family’s complicated entertainment system. Phil makes fun of her and claims that the remote is easy to use. Claire challenges him to teach Haley, expecting Haley to fail too. Instead, Haley learns it quickly. Claire then secretly asks Haley to teach her because she does not want Phil to know that he was right.

In the third storyline, Gloria arranges a blind date for Manny and helps him dress for it. When Whitney arrives, she mistakes Cameron for her date and clearly enjoys talking to him more than to Manny. Manny is disappointed, but he tries to handle the situation with dignity.

Overall, the episode is about pride and gradual change. Nobody becomes a new person overnight. However, admitting a weakness or making a small effort to understand someone else can still be meaningful.

## 4. 本集可迁移口语表达

- **fully accept something** — 完全接受某事
- **respond in a supportive way** — 以支持的态度回应
- **make fun of someone** — 取笑某人
- **handle something with dignity** — 体面地处理某事
- **change overnight** — 一夜之间改变

### 建议练习

1. 先看中文，确认人物关系和故事顺序。
2. 跟读英文标准版，重点模仿停顿和连接方式，不要逐句翻译。
3. 只看 Cue Version 完整复述；卡住时先绕开表达，不立刻查看原文。
4. 复述结束后对照标准版，只修改影响表达清晰度的问题。
