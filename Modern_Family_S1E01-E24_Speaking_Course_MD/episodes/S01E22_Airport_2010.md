# S01E22 — Airport 2010

> 用途：中文理解剧情 → 学习 Spoken English 标准版 → 使用 Cue Version 脱稿复述  
> 英文长度：约 **280 词**；正常学习语速约 **1.9–2.3 分钟**

---

## 1. 中文剧情描述

这一集是全家前往夏威夷旅行的开始。格洛丽亚原本告诉杰伊，这是给他的生日礼物，杰伊以为两个人会单独度假。到了机场，他才发现格洛丽亚把整个家庭都请来了。杰伊表面上抱怨人太多，心里却更担心：格洛丽亚是不是不愿意和自己单独相处。后来他才知道，其他家人会提前回去，格洛丽亚特意为两个人保留了几天私人时间。

米切尔到机场后发现钱包和证件落在家里，并把责任推给卡梅隆。菲尔主动开车带他回去取。克莱尔因此很生气，因为她害怕坐飞机，希望菲尔能留在身边，却又一直等着丈夫自己察觉。她和卡梅隆聊天后意识到，不能总要求伴侣读懂自己的心。与此同时，菲尔也告诉米切尔，有时伴侣虽然没有开口，自己仍应该主动注意对方的需要。两组人最后都向伴侣道歉。

机场里还发生了许多小插曲。曼尼的名字与禁飞名单上的人相同，被安保人员带去询问；海莉和一个男孩调情，后来才发现对方只有十四岁；迪伦前一晚在海莉房间睡着，全家出门后被防盗系统锁在房子里。

这一集把家庭旅行最混乱的阶段集中在登机前，也说明沟通既不能完全依赖“你应该懂我”，也不能只用“你没有说”来逃避关心。

## 2. Spoken English 标准版

I’d like to introduce Episode 22 of *Modern Family* Season 1. It’s called **“Airport 2010,”** and it follows the family as they try to leave for a vacation in Hawaii.

Gloria tells Jay that the trip is a birthday present. Jay expects a quiet vacation for the two of them, but at the airport he discovers that Gloria has invited the entire family. He complains about the crowd, but his deeper concern is that Gloria may be avoiding time alone with him. Later, he learns that everyone else will return home early. Gloria has actually planned several private days for the two of them at the end of the trip.

Meanwhile, Mitchell realizes that he left his wallet and identification at home. He blames Cameron for not packing them. Phil offers to drive Mitchell back to the house, which upsets Claire. She is afraid of flying and wanted Phil to stay with her, but she expected him to understand without being told. Cameron advises Claire to express her needs instead of asking Phil to read her mind. At the same time, Phil tells Mitchell that partners should sometimes notice when help is needed, even if nobody asks directly. Both couples eventually apologize.

Several smaller problems happen at the airport. Manny’s name matches a name on a no-fly list, so security questions him. Haley flirts with an attractive boy and later discovers that he is only fourteen. Dylan, meanwhile, is trapped inside the Dunphy house after falling asleep in Haley’s room the night before.

Overall, the episode is about communication. A partner cannot read every thought, but “you never asked” is not always a good excuse for failing to pay attention.

## 3. Cue Version

### Opening
- family tries to leave for Hawaii / airport chaos

### Jay and Gloria
- birthday trip → Jay expects private vacation
- discovers whole family is coming
- worries Gloria avoids being alone with him
- truth: family returns early / Gloria planned private days later

### Two couples and the wallet
- Mitchell forgets wallet and ID → blames Cameron
- Phil drives him home
- Claire is afraid of flying and wants Phil nearby
- expects him to read her mind
- Cameron: express your needs
- Phil: partners should also notice unspoken needs
- both couples apologize

### Airport problems
- Manny matches name on no-fly list
- Haley flirts with boy → he is fourteen
- Dylan trapped in Dunphy house

### Closing
- communication needs both speaking clearly and paying attention

## 4. 本集可迁移口语表达

- **avoid spending time alone with someone** — 避免与某人单独相处
- **read someone’s mind** — 猜透某人的心思
- **express one’s needs** — 表达自己的需要
- **pay attention** — 留意、关注
- **be held for questioning** — 被留下接受询问

### 建议练习

1. 先看中文，确认人物关系和故事顺序。
2. 跟读英文标准版，重点模仿停顿和连接方式，不要逐句翻译。
3. 只看 Cue Version 完整复述；卡住时先绕开表达，不立刻查看原文。
4. 复述结束后对照标准版，只修改影响表达清晰度的问题。
