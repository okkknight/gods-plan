# S01E16 — Fears

> 用途：中文理解剧情 → 学习 Spoken English 标准版 → 使用 Cue Version 脱稿复述  
> 英文长度：约 **293 词**；正常学习语速约 **2.0–2.4 分钟**

## 1. 中文剧情描述

这一集把每个家庭成员的恐惧放到了一起。菲尔害怕进入家里的狭窄爬行空间，却不愿在卢克面前承认。卢克把遥控车开了进去，自己又被困在里面，菲尔只好克服恐惧进去救他。为了让自己显得更勇敢，他还把里面的假骨头当成真正的危险，结果把事情弄得更加可笑。

克莱尔陪海莉参加驾照考试。她担心女儿失败，却又努力不把紧张表现出来。海莉最终通过考试。与此同时，亚历克斯害怕在学校舞会上跳舞，克莱尔鼓励她不要因为害怕出丑就错过体验。

米切尔和卡梅隆担心莉莉开口说的第一个词会是“妈妈”，因为家里并没有母亲。他们听见莉莉不断发出类似“Mommy”的声音后十分不安，后来才发现声音来自一个玩具。儿科医生也提醒他们，不需要把每个普通现象都理解成对家庭结构的否定。

杰伊嘲笑曼尼害怕坐过山车，格洛丽亚于是想办法把这两个同样胆小却不肯承认的人骗上了游乐设施。两个人一路强装镇定，直到真正开始后同时崩溃。

这一集的主题不是“勇敢的人没有恐惧”，而是每个人都有害怕的东西。勇敢只是愿意在必要的时候继续向前，哪怕表现得并不漂亮。

## 2. Spoken English 标准版

I’d like to introduce Episode 16 of *Modern Family* Season 1. It’s called **“Fears,”** and each storyline focuses on something a character is afraid of.

Phil is terrified of the narrow crawl space under the house, but he does not want Luke to know. When Luke’s remote-control car gets stuck inside and Luke goes after it, Phil has to face his fear and crawl in to help him. He tries to act brave, even after finding what he thinks are human bones. The “danger” turns out to be harmless, but Phil still proves that he can move forward when Luke needs him.

Claire takes Haley to her driving test. She worries that Haley will fail, but Haley passes. Claire also encourages Alex to attend a school dance. Alex is afraid of looking awkward, and Claire tells her that avoiding every embarrassing experience is not the same as being safe.

Meanwhile, Mitchell and Cameron worry that Lily’s first word will be “Mommy,” because there is no mother in their home. When they hear a voice repeatedly saying something that sounds like the word, they become anxious about what it means. They eventually discover that the sound comes from a toy. Their pediatrician reminds them that they do not need to treat every normal event as a judgment on their family.

Jay makes fun of Manny for being afraid of a roller coaster. Gloria knows that Jay is frightened too, so she tricks both of them into going on the ride. They pretend to be calm until the roller coaster starts, and then they panic together.

Overall, the episode suggests that courage does not mean having no fear. It means doing what is necessary even when you are frightened and do not look especially heroic.

- Gloria knows Jay is afraid too
- tricks both onto ride → both panic

## 4. 本集可迁移口语表达

- **face one’s fear** — 面对恐惧
- **act brave** — 装得很勇敢
- **be afraid of looking awkward** — 害怕显得尴尬
- **treat something as a judgment** — 把某事理解成一种评判
- **move forward** — 继续向前

### 建议练习

2. 跟读英文标准版，重点模仿停顿和连接方式，不要逐句翻译。
3. 只看 Cue Version 完整复述；卡住时先绕开表达，不立刻查看原文。
4. 复述结束后对照标准版，只修改影响表达清晰度的问题。
